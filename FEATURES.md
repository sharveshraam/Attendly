# Attendly — complete feature inventory

Everything requested is in scope: smart timetable and academic-calendar upload; OCR/AI extraction with human review; instructional/non-instructional day logic; fixed and rolling semester modes; per-course attendance and thresholds; bunk calculator; recovery planner; what-if simulator; attendance forecasting; schedule conflict detection; calendar day/week/month/agenda/semester views; drag/drop; add/cancel/replace/reschedule; recurring exceptions; timetable-vs-actual comparison; version history and undo; ICS import/export; group workspaces, invitations, roles, polls, approvals, activity feed and audit history; private personal attendance; notifications; PWA; offline attendance queue; push; AI assistant and natural-language schedule proposals; assignments; exams; notes/resources; heatmaps; risk ranking; reports; multi-semester history; command palette; keyboard shortcuts; customizable dashboards; themes; privacy mode; accessibility; backups; security; testing and monitoring.

The implementation must preserve the key invariant: shared schedule data is separate from private attendance data.
